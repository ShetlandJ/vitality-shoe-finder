Vitality Shoe Finder

Every in-stock shoe on the SportsShoes Vitality page, ranked by rating, adjusted for review count so a shoe with a single 5-star review doesn't beat one with 4.7 stars from 1,000 reviews. Prices checked {{UPDATED}}.

    How the ranking works. Each shoe's average is blended with {{M}} imaginary reviews at the catalogue average of {{MEAN}}★. A shoe with hundreds of reviews barely moves. A shoe with two reviews gets pulled most of the way to the average until more reviews come in. The plain star rating and review count are shown alongside.

    Prices. "With 50% code" is half the current price, as an estimate. The code is applied at checkout, so check the basket total. Shoes without the eligible tag are hidden by default; untick the box to see them.

    Stock is as of the date above and can sell out. Each colour link goes to the product page on sportsshoes.com.
